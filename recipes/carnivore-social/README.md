# Carnivore Social Recipe Collection

This folder contains original, concise recipe cards derived from recurring carnivore food ideas found in public social-media posts and cross-posts.

## Scope

Focus areas:
- smash burgers and cheeseburgers
- bacon / egg / cheese combinations
- carnivore buns and chaffles
- fakeaway breakfast sandwiches
- meatball subs and panini-style sandwiches
- high-flavour beef plates
- surf-and-turf meals
- meatza / carnivore pizza
- slow-cooked beef
- rich egg dishes

## Files

- `recipes.json` — original Reddit/cross-post social recipe cards for app use.
- `instagram-recipes.json` — Instagram-focused batch with direct or independently attributed Instagram sources, full ingredients and cooking instructions.
- `SOURCES.md` — original social source ledger and access notes.
- `INSTAGRAM-SOURCES.md` — source ledger for the Instagram-specific recipe batch.

## Source handling

Recipes are rewritten into original instructions rather than copying captions or transcripts. Reddit posts are used directly where public. Instagram/TikTok coverage is limited to publicly indexed material, creator indexes, Linktree profiles, independently attributed recipes, or recipes that creators cross-posted elsewhere. TikTok blocks normal web crawling, so this collection does not claim full TikTok coverage.

## Strict carnivore handling

Every recipe is written as a strict animal-food version. Plant condiments, sweeteners, flour/starch fillers and similar ingredients are omitted. Deli meat, sausage, pepperoni, bacon and hotdogs should be checked for sugar, starch and plant fillers.
