# Carnivore Social Recipe Collection

This folder contains original, concise recipe cards derived from recurring carnivore food ideas found in public social-media posts and cross-posts.

## Scope

Focus areas:
- smash burgers and cheeseburgers
- bacon / egg / cheese combinations
- carnivore buns and chaffles
- fakeaway breakfast sandwiches
- meatball subs and panini-style sandwiches
- high-flavour beef plates
- slow-cooked beef
- rich egg dishes

## Files

- `recipes.json` — structured recipe cards for app use.
- `SOURCES.md` — public source ledger and access notes.

## Source handling

Recipes are rewritten into original instructions rather than copying captions or transcripts. Reddit posts are used directly where public. Instagram/TikTok coverage is limited to publicly indexed material, creator indexes, Linktree profiles, or recipes that creators cross-posted to Reddit. TikTok blocks normal web crawling, so this collection does not claim full TikTok coverage.

## Strict carnivore handling

Every recipe is written as a strict animal-food version. Plant condiments, sweeteners, flour/starch fillers and similar ingredients are omitted. Deli meat, sausage and hotdogs should be checked for sugar, starch and plant fillers.
