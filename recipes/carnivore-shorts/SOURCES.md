## Notes

Search engines often canonicalise YouTube Shorts to standard `watch?v=` URLs, so `sourceFormat` is intentionally generic until a full channel/Shorts enumerator is connected. The recipe folder is designed so a later scraper can append additional entries without changing the app-facing schema.
